## 1. Current implementation

The current ScrollTell Studio build does not include advertising trackers, analytics SDKs, social pixels, or third-party embeds. The local development dashboard uses ordinary browser application state and a local API. The hosted production service must maintain an inventory before launch.

## 2. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small files stored on a device. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, SDK identifiers, pixels, and other methods that store or access information on a device. This Policy applies to all of them.

## 3. Essential technologies

We may use strictly necessary technologies for authentication sessions, CSRF protection, security, load balancing, privacy preferences, and core service operation. ## 4. Optional technologies

We will not activate analytics, advertising, personalization, or social-embed technologies until we have documented the purpose, provider, data categories, retention, transfer, and consent requirement. Optional technologies will be off by default where consent is required. Users will be able to accept, reject, or configure categories with equal clarity and change their choice later through “Privacy choices.”

## 5. Analytics

**Current status: not implemented.** If analytics is added, the service owner must decide whether the tool is necessary, configure it to minimize data, document the legal basis, and obtain consent where required. Analytics vendors and settings must be listed here before activation.

## 6. Third-party embeds

**Current status: not implemented.** External videos, social posts, fonts, maps, and support widgets can disclose IP addresses and device information to their providers. We will use a click-to-load or privacy-enhanced approach where appropriate, and will not load a non-essential embed before the required consent.

## 7. Consent and withdrawal

Where consent is required, a user must make a clear affirmative choice. Continuing to browse, a pre-ticked box, or hiding the choice in a privacy policy does not constitute consent. Refusal must be as easy as acceptance. A consent record will store the policy version, categories, timestamp, source, and relevant device or account reference without storing more information than necessary.

## 8. Browser controls

Users can also manage storage through browser settings. Blocking essential technologies may prevent sign-in or core service functions. Browser controls do not replace the service’s consent and preference controls where consent is legally required.
