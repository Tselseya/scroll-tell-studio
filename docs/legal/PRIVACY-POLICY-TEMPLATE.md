## 1. Who we are

[LEGAL ENTITY NAME] operates M.O.S.A.N.G., a hosted content-workspace service that helps users draft, organize, transform, review, and publish content through connected AI providers and social-media platforms. For personal data processed to provide the service, [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] acts as the personal information controller under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and, where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, as the controller or processor identified in the applicable service relationship.

## 2. Scope

This Policy applies to the M.O.S.A.N.G. website, application, support channels, and related services. It covers information about account holders, organization members, visitors, applicants, support contacts, and people whose information users place into the service. Users must not upload personal data unless they have a lawful basis and the authority to do so.

## 3. Information we collect

We collect account and identity information such as name, email address, login identifiers, organization membership, role, and account status. We collect service content such as drafts, platform variants, media metadata, publishing schedules, job records, prompts, and audit events. When a user connects a third-party account, we receive the account identifier, authorization scopes, tokens or token references, expiry information, and provider response data necessary to operate the integration. We collect support messages and security information such as IP address, device details, authentication events, and service logs.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless the user places it into content or a support request. Users should not upload health, financial-account, government-identification, children’s, or other sensitive information unless they have assessed the risks and have a lawful basis.

## 4. Purposes and legal bases

We process information to create and secure accounts, provide workspaces, store and synchronize content, execute user-requested AI transformations, connect authorized publishing accounts, queue and execute publishing jobs, provide support, prevent abuse, maintain service security, comply with law, and establish or defend legal claims. In the Philippines, our processing relies on applicable lawful bases under the Data Privacy Act, including consent, contract necessity, legal obligation, protection of vital interests where applicable, public-authority functions where applicable, and legitimate interests subject to the rights of data subjects. For users in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, we rely on the GDPR lawful basis stated for each processing purpose in our internal processing register. Optional analytics and marketing are based on consent where required.

We will not use content or connected-account data for a materially incompatible purpose without providing additional notice and, where required, obtaining consent.
